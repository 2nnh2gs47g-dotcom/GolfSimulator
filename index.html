What this can and cannot measure

Measured: ball speed and vertical launch angle, both from pixel displacement scaled by the ball's 42.67 mm diameter. Nothing else on this screen is estimated or modelled.

Not measured: spin, carry, curve, club speed. Those need phase 2 and they need an inference model, so they stay off this screen rather than appear as a guess.

The frame rate ceiling. A browser camera usually delivers 30–60 fps. A driver at 150 mph covers about 1.1 m per frame at 60 fps, so the ball leaves the frame before a second sample exists. Below roughly 120 fps this tool is honest only for wedges, chips and putts. Slower phone speeds and shorter clubs give more samples and tighter numbers. A native build with 240 fps slo-mo is what makes driver readings viable.

Angle discipline. Face-on to the ball, lens level, ball filling a reasonable part of the frame. Any tilt or off-axis placement biases both numbers, and the app cannot detect that for you.

Validating it. Roll a ball a measured distance across a flat floor at a steady speed and check the reading against distance over stopwatch time, or compare a few wedge shots against a known launch monitor. Do this before trusting any number.
